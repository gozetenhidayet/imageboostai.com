Product Photo Editor

Product Photo Editor wraps your picture in a clean presentation layout. It creates a white canvas, adds padding of 12% of the image width on each side and 8% of the height at the top, and draws a soft, blurred shadow under the picture. Below the picture it adds a mirrored reflection that is 32% of the image height, shown at 32% opacity and fading to white. The result is a catalog-style card.

Open Product Photo Editor

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

The output is larger than your input. For example, a 1000 × 1000 image becomes 1240 pixels wide and 1465 pixels tall. The tool does not remove the original background, so it works best with a transparent cutout or a product that already sits on a plain white or light background. The shadow and reflection are simple effects, not a physical rendering of light.

When to use Product Photo Editor

  • You want a consistent, clean card for a product listing or a catalog page.
  • You have a product cutout and want to give it a floor reflection and shadow quickly.
  • You are making a simple presentation image for a small online shop.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the picture. For best results, remove the background with Remove Background and export the cutout.
  2. Upload the cutout or a product on a light background and open Product Photo Editor.
  3. Press Apply Tool.
  4. Inspect the reflection and shadow. They should be subtle and align with the bottom edge of the product.
  5. Download the result and use Resize Image if the marketplace requires a specific size.

Tips for better results

  • A square product photo gives a balanced card. Crop to 1:1 first for a neat result.
  • If the original has a gray or colored background, the reflection will copy it and look odd against the white canvas. Use a cutout.
  • Apply the tool last. Edits made afterward, such as sharpening, also affect the white margins.
  • Check marketplace rules. Some require pure white backgrounds and no reflections or shadows.

Common mistakes

  • Applying the tool to a photo with a busy background, which adds a reflection of the busy background.
  • Expecting an automatic background removal. The tool only adds the white canvas, shadow and reflection.
  • Uploading the result to a marketplace that prohibits added effects.

What to expect

This presentation effect does not automatically remove the original background. Use Remove Background first when appropriate.

A transparent product cutout can become a simple catalog card with consistent white space.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Models and libraries, where a tool needs them, download from public content networks. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Questions about Product Photo Editor

How big will the result be?

Width becomes the original width plus 24%, and height becomes about 146.5% of the original, so a 1000 × 1000 image becomes 1240 × 1465.

Does it remove the background?

No. It adds a white canvas around the image. Use Remove Background first for a clean cutout.

Can I remove the reflection?

There is no option to turn it off. If you do not want it, crop it away with Crop Image after applying the tool, or use Background Changer for a plain backdrop instead.
