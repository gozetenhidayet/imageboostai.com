Photo Collage Maker

Photo Collage Maker combines your current image with up to eight additional JPG, PNG or WebP files into one grid. You choose two or three columns, an output size from 100 to 6000 pixels on each side (default 1600 × 1600), a gap between cells from 0 to 100 pixels (default 16), a background color and a fit mode. Each added file can be up to 25 MB, and the additional images may total no more than 24 megapixels.

Open Photo Collage Maker

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

Fill cells crops each image to cover its cell, so edges may be cut off. Fit whole images keeps the entire picture visible and fills any leftover space with the background color. The extra images appear in a list where you can move them up or down or remove them before you press Create collage. If the last row is not full, its empty cells simply show the background.

When to use Photo Collage Maker

  • You want to show several angles of a product in one image.
  • You are making a before-and-after or a set of event photos for a social post.
  • You need a simple grid for a presentation slide.

Step by step

  1. Upload your first image, which becomes the first cell, and open Photo Collage Maker.
  2. Use Additional images to add up to eight more files, and reorder them with the arrow buttons.
  3. Choose the number of columns, the output width and height, the gap, the fit mode and the background color.
  4. Press Create collage and check that no important part of any picture is cut off.
  5. If the collage is wrong, change the settings and press Create collage again, then download the result.

Tips for better results

  • Choose an output ratio that matches your cells. A 1600 × 1600 two-column collage with four images has square cells of 776 pixels, so square photos fit without cropping.
  • Use Fit whole images when pictures have different shapes and you do not want to lose any content.
  • A gap of 8 to 24 pixels with a white or neutral background gives a clean look.
  • Make the collage the last step. Edit each photo individually before combining.

Common mistakes

  • Using Fill cells with portrait and landscape photos mixed together, which crops heads and edges.
  • Making the gap so large that the cells become too small. If the gap is too big for the size, the tool asks you to reduce it.
  • Adding very large files. Resize them first if the combined size exceeds the 24-megapixel limit.

What to expect

Fill mode crops edges; Fit mode retains the whole image and may leave margins. An incomplete final row keeps the background visible.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about Photo Collage Maker

How many photos can I include?

Your current image plus up to eight additional images, for a total of nine. Each additional file can be up to 25 MB and the extra images must total no more than 24 megapixels.

How is the cell size calculated?

The width and height minus the gaps are divided by the number of columns and rows. With 1600 pixels, a 16-pixel gap and two columns, each cell is (1600 − 16 × 3) ÷ 2 = 776 pixels wide.

Can I use different layouts, such as one big photo and several small ones?

Not in this tool. It creates an even grid with two or three columns.
