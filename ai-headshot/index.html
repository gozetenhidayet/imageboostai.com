AI Headshot

AI Headshot is a portrait-styling shortcut that uses ordinary image processing and a subject cutout, not a generative model. Pressing Apply Tool cuts out the person with the same segmentation step as Remove Background, places them on a soft vertical gradient that runs from pale lavender to a slightly deeper blue-lavender, and lifts brightness slightly. This gives a plain studio-style backdrop.

Open AI Headshot

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

The panel also offers an illustrated attire overlay. You choose Navy Suit, Black Blazer or Business Casual and set the Shoulder position slider from 50% to 90% of the image height (default 70%). The tool then draws a flat jacket shape with lapels in a dark navy, black or charcoal tone from that line down to the bottom of the image. It is a simple illustration, clearly different from real clothing, and it does not generate a new identity or a professional photoshoot.

When to use AI Headshot

  • You need a quick plain background for a profile picture and the original background is distracting.
  • You want to preview how a dark jacket color looks at the bottom of a casual portrait, with the understanding that it is an illustration.
  • You are preparing an informal avatar and do not need a photorealistic result.

Step by step

  1. Upload a front-facing portrait with the head and shoulders clearly visible and a good separation from the background.
  2. Press Apply Tool to cut out the subject and place it on the studio gradient, then inspect the edges at 100% zoom.
  3. If you want the illustrated attire, choose a style and adjust the Shoulder position slider so that the top of the jacket sits where the shoulders meet the neck area.
  4. Press Apply Professional Attire and view the result at the size you plan to use.
  5. Use Undo if the illustration looks out of place, then download the result.

Tips for better results

  • The attire overlay is a flat shape. It looks best at small sizes, such as a profile thumbnail, and looks obviously drawn at larger sizes.
  • For a professional context, a real photo in real clothing is usually better than any overlay.
  • A plain, evenly lit original gives the cleanest cutout. Busy backgrounds can leave a rim around hair.
  • Adjust the shoulder position so the jacket does not cover the chin or neck.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting the tool to generate realistic clothing, hands or a different pose.
  • Using the overlay on a picture where the person is not centered or not facing the camera.
  • Presenting an illustrated overlay as a real photograph in a context where authenticity matters, such as an ID or a professional directory.

What to expect

It does not generate a new identity, realistic clothing or a professional photoshoot from a prompt.

Use a simple studio background for a profile image; the attire overlay remains visibly illustrated.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Models and libraries, where a tool needs them, download from public content networks. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Questions about AI Headshot

Is this AI-generated?

Not in the generative sense. A segmentation model finds the subject. The background and the attire are fixed gradients and flat shapes that the tool draws. Nothing about your face or clothing is synthesized.

Can I use the result for an official ID photo?

No. This tool is not designed for identity documents, which have strict requirements. For size presets, see Resize Image, and always check the issuing authority's rules.

Why does the jacket look flat?

It is a drawn geometric overlay with a simple gradient, not a photograph. That is the intended behavior, and it is why the page calls it illustrated.
