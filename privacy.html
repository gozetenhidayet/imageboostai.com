Privacy Policy

Updated November 6, 2026.

Images and editing sessions

The included photo editor processes selected image content in your browser. This release does not send your photos to a paid generative AI backend. Images, previews and undo history are held in the browser session; closing or resetting the editor clears its working state. Downloaded files are stored wherever you choose on your device.

Libraries and models

OCR, HEIC decoding and foreground recognition can download software or model files from third-party hosts including jsDelivr, cdnjs and TensorFlow model hosting. Ordinary network requests can reveal IP address, browser information and the requested resource. Browser caching can retain downloaded resources.

Preferences and advertising

The main site can store language, theme, favorites, notice dismissal and consent preferences in browser storage. Optional analytics and advertising services are controlled by the site's consent settings. Google advertising may use cookies or similar technologies as described in Google's partner-site information. Regional consent requirements and any certified consent platform are managed by the site operator.

Contact

Emails you send contain the information you choose to include. Contact contact@imageboostai.com about privacy or data requests. Avoid including sensitive images unless specifically necessary.
