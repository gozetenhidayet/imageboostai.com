Privacy Policy

Short version: your photos are edited in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers. This page explains the details, including the third-party services that can be contacted while you use the site.

Updated November 11, 2026.

Your images

The photo editor processes the images you choose in your browser. Images, previews and undo history are held in the browser session. Closing or resetting the editor clears them. Downloaded files are saved wherever you choose on your own device. We do not receive, store or review your photos, and we do not use them to train any model.

Third-party services and downloads

Some features need software or data that is fetched from public services when you use them. Those services receive an ordinary network request, which includes your IP address, browser information and the address of the file requested, as for any website.

The guide pages (tool guides, articles, About, Terms and this page) are static pages. They load no scripts and no external fonts.

Cookies and browser storage

The main editor page stores preferences in your browser’s local storage and cookies: your language, theme, favorite tools, whether you dismissed a notice, and your consent choices. These stay on your device. The interface language of the editor is switched with translations built into the page, so no page text is sent to a translation service. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

Analytics and advertising

The main editor page can use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and understand which tools are used, and Google AdSense to show advertising. Both are optional. They are off by default, and the page loads them only if you choose “Accept all” or enable them in “Manage preferences” in the consent banner. You can change your choice later from the same place. Choosing “Reject non-essential” keeps them off.

The site can also load Microsoft Clarity analytics, again only with consent. At the time of writing it is not configured and does not run. At the time of writing, the site is not displaying advertisements. If advertising is shown in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s information about how it uses data from partner sites. Where required, advertising consent will be requested first.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs, for example IP addresses, as described in its own privacy statement. We do not have access to a log of individual visitors’ images, because images are never sent to the site.

Email you send us

The contact page opens your email application; it does not collect anything through a form. If you email us, we receive the address and message you choose to send, and we use it only to reply, fix a problem or correct a guide. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or sensitive personal photographs.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your photos or an account for you, there is little personal data for us to access, correct or delete. If you emailed us and want your messages deleted, or you want to ask about data held about you, write to contact@imageboostai.com. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act, including to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to certain processing. Requests to Google or other third parties about data they collect must be made to them.

Children

The site is a general-purpose tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works in a way that affects privacy, for example by adding a service, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@imageboostai.com.
