Eye Enhancer

Eye Enhancer makes the painted region more vivid. Inside your selection, the tool blends 80% of a filtered copy into the original. The filter raises contrast by 30%, saturation by 25% and brightness by 8%, which makes the iris more distinct and brings out detail in the eye.

Open Eye Enhancer

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

The brush runs from 8 to 90 on the preview scale (default 28) and the panel includes an Eraser and a Clear button. The tool does not detect eyes, and it does not restore focus. If the eyes are blurry in the original, enhancing them will only make a blurry eye more colorful.

When to use Eye Enhancer

  • A portrait has dull or slightly dark eyes that you want to make more noticeable.
  • The iris color looks washed out because of flat lighting.
  • You want a subtle lift for a profile photo.

Step by step

  1. Upload a portrait and zoom so that the eyes fill a good part of the screen.
  2. Open Eye Enhancer and choose a small brush, around 8 to 20 on the size slider.
  3. Paint over the iris and the visible detail of each eye, avoiding the skin and eyelashes around it.
  4. Press Apply and view both eyes together to check that they match in brightness and color.
  5. Use Undo if the eyes look unnatural, then download the result.

Tips for better results

  • Do the same selection for both eyes so they stay balanced.
  • Keep the pupil and the catch-light, the small reflection in the eye, visible. Over-brightening removes them and makes the eyes look flat.
  • A very small brush gives the best control and avoids changing the skin tone around the eyes.
  • Check the result at the size the picture will be viewed. Small changes look larger when zoomed in.

Common mistakes

  • Painting the eyelids and skin, which makes the surrounding skin more saturated and contrasted than the rest of the face.
  • Treating only one eye, which leads to a mismatch.
  • Using the tool on a photo where eyes are closed or out of focus.

What to expect

The filter does not understand eye anatomy or restore missing focus. Avoid painting the skin around the eye.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about Eye Enhancer

Can it change eye color?

No. It increases contrast, saturation and brightness in the painted area but does not change the hue. For a color change, use Object Replace on the iris.

Does it fix red-eye?

No. It is not designed for red-eye correction and may make a red tint more intense.

Does it detect eyes?

No. It applies the same filter to whatever you paint.
