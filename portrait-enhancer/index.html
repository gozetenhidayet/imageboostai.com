Portrait Enhancer

Portrait Enhancer is a one-click adjustment for photos of people. It first identifies skin-colored pixels with a simple color test, then blends 42% of a 3.2-pixel blur into those pixels to soften texture. Afterward, it applies a small tone adjustment to the whole image: it raises red, green and blue slightly (by 4%, 3% and 2%) and lifts the darkest areas by up to 10 levels, which opens up shadows without changing bright areas.

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

The skin test is based on color, not on recognizing a face, so it can also affect warm-colored non-skin areas such as wood, sand or a tan wall. There are no sliders. The effect is mild and designed to be applied once.

When to use Portrait Enhancer

  • You want a quick overall improvement for a portrait without choosing settings.
  • A portrait looks slightly flat in the shadows and a bit rough on the skin.
  • You want a starting point before using brush-based retouching for finer control.

Step by step

  1. Upload the portrait and open Portrait Enhancer.
  2. Press Apply Tool once.
  3. Compare skin, hair, background and clothing with the original. Look for areas that changed but should not have, such as a wooden shelf.
  4. If the background changed unnaturally, use Undo and retouch with Face Retouch, which affects only the painted area.
  5. Download the result.

Tips for better results

  • The tool works best on photos where the background is not skin-toned. Gray, blue or green backgrounds are safest.
  • Run it once. Because skin texture is blended again each time, a second pass gives a smoother, more artificial look.
  • For profile pictures, view the result at a small size, which is how it will usually be seen.
  • For portraits with strong colored lighting, adjust color first with Color Correction.

Common mistakes

  • Using it on photos with large warm-colored areas that are not skin, which soften along with the face.
  • Expecting detailed retouching. This is a light, uniform adjustment.
  • Applying it repeatedly until the skin looks plastic.

What to expect

Color-based skin heuristics are imperfect across lighting and skin tones; compare the entire image.

A warm indoor portrait may benefit from a small adjustment, but matching background colors can also be affected.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about Portrait Enhancer

Does Portrait Enhancer use face detection?

No. It identifies skin by color, which can be imperfect with different lighting and skin tones, so always compare the whole image with the original.

How is it different from Skin Smoothing?

Both use a color-based skin test. Skin Smoothing's Quick Smooth blends 55% of a 4-pixel blur and adds Heal Spot for blemishes. Portrait Enhancer blends less (42%), uses a 3.2-pixel blur, and also adjusts overall tone.

Can I control the strength?

There is no slider. To reduce the effect, use Face Retouch on a smaller painted area instead.
