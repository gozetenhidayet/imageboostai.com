AI Background Generator

Despite its name, AI Background Generator is a preset background studio. It uses the DeepLab-based cutout from Remove Background and composites your subject over a curated radial color gradient. It does not run a text-to-image model and it does not invent scenery. The name is kept for people who still search for it under that term.

Open AI Background Generator

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

Six presets are available: Studio Cyan, Sunset, Forest, Gold Hour, Cool Slate and Rose Studio. Each one is a three-stop radial gradient with a lighter highlight toward the upper right. If you want a flat color or your own image, use Background Changer instead.

When to use AI Background Generator

  • You want a quick, even studio-style backdrop behind a portrait for a profile picture.
  • You are comparing a handful of presets to see which gives your product the best contrast.
  • You prefer a ready-made backdrop to choosing colors yourself.

Step by step

  1. Upload a photo with a clearly visible subject.
  2. Open AI Background Generator and click one of the six presets. It applies immediately.
  3. Wait for the subject to be recognized and composited. The first run downloads the model.
  4. Look at the outline against the lighter and darker parts of the gradient and note any halo.
  5. Use Undo and try a second preset if the first one reduces contrast, then download the version you prefer.

Tips for better results

  • A cool gradient makes warm-toned subjects stand out; a warm gradient does the opposite.
  • Dark presets reveal bright fringes left over from the original background, so inspect them before exporting.
  • Use Undo between presets instead of applying them on top of each other.
  • If the edges are rough, fix the cutout first with a better source photo rather than trying more presets.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting a generated scene such as a beach or an office. The presets are gradients.
  • Applying several presets in a row, which stacks the compositing steps and degrades the edge.
  • Judging the result only at thumbnail size. Edge defects appear when you view the image at 100%.

What to expect

Despite the legacy name, this is a preset background studio, not text-to-image generation.

A cool gradient can separate a warm-colored product; a dark preset exposes unwanted light halos.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Models and libraries, where a tool needs them, download from public content networks. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Questions about AI Background Generator

Is this really AI?

It uses a machine-learning model only for finding the subject. The backdrop itself is a fixed set of gradients, so nothing is generated. We keep the original name only because many users search for it.

Can I upload my own background here?

Use Background Changer for that. It supports solid colors, gradients and your own image files.

Why are there light edges around my subject?

The cutout is slightly larger than the true subject in places and keeps some of the old background color. A darker backdrop makes that rim easier to see. Choose a lighter preset or start from a photo with cleaner separation.
