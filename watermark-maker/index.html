Watermark Maker

Watermark Maker places visible text or a logo on your image. For text, type the words and the tool draws them in bold white Arial with a dark outline, sized at about one twenty-second of the image width. For a logo, choose an image file; it is scaled to 18% of the image width. Opacity runs from 10% to 100% and starts at 40%. Position choices are Bottom Right, Bottom Left, Top Right and Center, with a 2% margin from the edges.

Open Watermark Maker

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

A Tile checkbox repeats the mark across the entire photo at a slight angle, which is harder to crop out. If you leave the text box empty and no logo is chosen, the tool uses the default text © ImageBoostAI, so type your own text first. A watermark is a visible deterrent and a credit line. It is not copy protection and it can be removed by determined editors.

When to use Watermark Maker

  • You publish photos online and want a visible credit or brand mark on them.
  • You send draft images to a client and want to prevent unlicensed use of the preview.
  • You want a consistent logo placement on a series of product photos.

Step by step

  1. Upload the image and open Watermark Maker.
  2. Type your watermark text, or press Choose Logo and select a PNG with a transparent background.
  3. Set Opacity and choose a position. Tick Tile across the whole photo for a repeated mark.
  4. Press Add Watermark and check the result at the size the image will be viewed.
  5. Download. Keep an unwatermarked original for your own records.

Tips for better results

  • A transparent PNG logo gives cleaner edges than a logo with a white box around it.
  • For preview images, use Tile with an opacity of 20 to 30%. It is visible but still lets viewers see the photo.
  • Check legibility on both light and dark areas. White text with a dark outline works in most places, but a logo may need a different position.
  • Use a corner position for a credit line on final images, and a tiled mark only for proofs.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the text box empty and publishing the default © ImageBoostAI text by accident.
  • Using a high-opacity watermark in the center of a photo you want people to enjoy.
  • Treating the watermark as security. Anyone can crop, repair or recreate the image.

What to expect

A watermark is a visible deterrent, not copy protection. Transparent PNG logos usually give cleaner edges.

Place a low-opacity logo in the bottom-right corner and confirm it remains legible at the final display size.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Models and libraries, where a tool needs them, download from public content networks. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Questions about Watermark Maker

Can I change the font or color of the watermark text?

Not in this tool. The text is always bold Arial in white with a dark outline, which stays legible on most backgrounds. For other fonts, use Add Text to Photo.

What size is the logo?

It is scaled to 18% of the image width and keeps its proportions.

How do I remove the default text?

Type your own text before pressing Add Watermark. Use Undo if you applied the default text by mistake.
