Exact dimensions · Browser-based workflow

Resize Image

Resize Image changes the pixel dimensions of a photo. Enter a width and a height in whole pixels, keep Lock Aspect Ratio on to prevent stretching, and press Resize. The picture is resampled with the browser's high-quality smoothing. Six one-click presets are included: 1920×1080, 1280×720, 1080×1080, 800×600, 400×400 and 1200×630.

Open Resize Image

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

The panel also contains photo size presets for twelve countries, calculated at a nominal 300 DPI: United States and India (2×2 in, 600×600 px), United Kingdom, Schengen/EU, Australia, Japan and Russia (35×45 mm, 413×531 px), Canada and Brazil (50×70 mm, 591×827 px), China (33×48 mm, 390×567 px), Nigeria (38×38 mm, 449×449 px) and Saudi Arabia (40×60 mm, 472×709 px). A preset first crops your photo from the center to the correct shape, so faces are not stretched, and then scales it to the exact pixel size. Requirements change and differ by purpose, so always confirm them with the issuing authority.

When to use Resize Image

  • A website, form or marketplace requires an exact pixel size.
  • A photo is too large to upload or to email and needs smaller dimensions.
  • You need a passport-style crop at the nominal size for a country and want the proportions to be right.

Step by step

  1. Upload the image and open Resize Image. The width and height fields show the current size.
  2. Enter a new width or height. With the aspect lock on, the other value updates automatically.
  3. Press Resize, or press one of the presets to fill in a common size first.
  4. For a photo-size preset, choose a country from the list and press Apply. Check the framing afterward.
  5. Download the result. Both sides may not exceed 16,384 pixels and the total may not exceed 40 megapixels.

Tips for better results

  • Reduce size rather than enlarge when you can. Making an image smaller keeps it sharp; making it larger softens it.
  • Crop to the right proportions first, then resize. Resizing alone cannot change the shape without stretching.
  • After a large reduction, a light pass of Image Sharpener can restore crispness.
  • For a passport-style photo, position the face in the center before applying the preset, since the crop is taken from the middle of the image.

Common mistakes

  • Turning off the aspect lock and entering both dimensions without checking the ratio, which stretches the picture.
  • Using the country preset as a guarantee of compliance. Required background, head size and file type differ, and the preset only sets the pixel size and shape.
  • Entering decimal values. Dimensions must be whole numbers.

What to expect

Both dimensions are limited to 16,384 pixels and the total output to 40 megapixels.

A 2400 × 1600 image resized proportionally to width 1200 becomes 1200 × 800.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about Resize Image

What does the 300 DPI note mean?

The preset sizes are the printed size in millimeters or inches converted to pixels at 300 pixels per inch. For example, 2 × 2 in at 300 DPI is 600 × 600 pixels.

Will a country preset make my photo valid for a passport?

Not by itself. It sets the pixel dimensions and shape. Requirements such as the background color, head size and expression are set by the authority, and they change over time.

What are the size limits?

Each side can be up to 16,384 pixels and the total up to 40 megapixels. Larger values are rejected with a message.
