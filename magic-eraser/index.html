Magic Eraser

Magic Eraser opens the same panel as Object Remover and AI Inpainting: a Brush, a Lasso, an Eraser to undo mask strokes, a Clear button and a Remove Selected Area button. Whatever you mark is filled with colors taken from the pixels next to it, so the strengths and limits are identical: clean on simple surfaces, visibly smooth on complex ones.

Open Magic Eraser

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

The word magic can suggest that the software understands what you are erasing. It does not. It treats your stroke as a mask and averages the surrounding colors into it, which is why a small brush and short strokes work best. We list all three names because people search for each of them, and we say plainly that they are one engine.

When to use Magic Eraser

  • You need to take out a dust speck, a small spot or a stray mark from a studio backdrop.
  • You want a fast fix for a mark on a screenshot or a diagram.
  • You are cleaning up a scan before using other tools such as Image Sharpener.

Step by step

  1. Upload the photo and open Magic Eraser.
  2. Zoom to the mark and paint over it with short brush strokes. Reduce the brush size for fine marks.
  3. Press Remove Selected Area and compare the result with the original.
  4. Make a second pass only if the first one left a visible trace. Use Undo if the fill loses detail.
  5. Download the cleaned image.

Tips for better results

  • Use the smallest brush that fully covers the mark. The size slider runs from 8 to 90 on the preview scale.
  • Work along a scratch in several short strokes instead of one long stroke, and fill each section before moving on.
  • Check the result at 100% and at the size you will publish. A patch that looks fine small can reveal itself when enlarged.
  • If an area looks too smooth, a small amount of image noise added later in another editor will help it blend.

Common mistakes

  • Erasing large objects. The tool cannot rebuild the background behind them.
  • Brushing across a sharp edge, which smears the edge into the fill.
  • Repeating passes over the same spot until it forms a visible blur.

What to expect

Complex textures may smear. Keep the original and use Undo when the repair loses detail.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about Magic Eraser

What is the difference between Magic Eraser, Object Remover and AI Inpainting?

Nothing in the engine. All three open the same panel and run the same boundary-fill repair. The names exist because people look for them under different terms.

Can I undo a pass?

Yes. The editor keeps up to 12 steps of history in the current browser session, and you can undo and redo them.

Why do some areas look smudged?

The fill averages neighboring colors, which removes texture. Smaller strokes and simpler surroundings give better results.
