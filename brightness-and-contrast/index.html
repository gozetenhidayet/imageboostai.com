Brightness & Contrast

Brightness & Contrast offers three independent sliders, Brightness, Contrast and Saturation, each from 0% to 200%. A setting of 100% is neutral for each one, so you only change the sliders you need. The adjustment is applied with the browser's standard image filters, which makes it fast and predictable.

Open Brightness & Contrast

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

Saturation at 0% produces a grayscale image, and 200% doubles color intensity. Zero is a valid setting for every slider, so setting Brightness to 0 produces a black image. Extreme values clip highlights and shadows, and the tool cannot restore detail that is already clipped in the original.

When to use Brightness & Contrast

  • A photo is a little dark or washed out and needs a quick fix.
  • You want a grayscale version of a color photo.
  • You need to increase contrast so text on a scanned page is easier to read.

Step by step

  1. Upload the image and open Brightness & Contrast.
  2. Leave sliders you do not need at 100%. Move Brightness up or down in small steps first.
  3. Adjust Contrast to separate darks and lights, then Saturation if colors look dull or too intense.
  4. Press Apply and check bright areas for loss of detail.
  5. Download the result or use Undo to try again.

Tips for better results

  • For a grayscale conversion, keep Brightness and Contrast at 100% and set Saturation to 0%.
  • Small changes of 5 to 15 points are usually enough for photographs.
  • Increase Contrast before Brightness when scanning documents. A white page often needs contrast more than light.
  • To see clipping, zoom in on the brightest areas, such as clouds, after applying.

Common mistakes

  • Raising Brightness to fix an underexposed photo. It brightens everything, including noise. Try Color Correction's Shadows slider instead.
  • Setting Contrast too high, which turns subtle tones to solid white and black.
  • Forgetting that 100% is neutral and entering a small value like 10, which makes the image almost black.

What to expect

Overly high settings clip highlights and shadows. The tool cannot restore already clipped image data.

Keep brightness and contrast at 100%, set saturation to 0%, then compare the grayscale output.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about Brightness & Contrast

What does 100% mean?

It means no change. Values below 100% reduce the property and values above 100% increase it, up to 200%.

How do I make a black-and-white photo?

Set Saturation to 0% and leave Brightness and Contrast at 100%, then press Apply.

Why did a small number make my photo almost black?

The sliders are percentages of the original. A Brightness of 10% keeps only a tenth of the original brightness. Use values near 100% for gentle changes.
