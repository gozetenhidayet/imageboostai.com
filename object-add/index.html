Object Add

Object Add places an emoji sticker on your image. You choose one of ten graphics (sparkles, a star, a heart, fire, a party popper, sunglasses, a crown, a rainbow, a yellow star and a butterfly), set its horizontal and vertical position as percentages of the image (defaults 72% and 28%), adjust its size (default 8%) and press Place Sticker. You can apply the tool again to add more stickers. The sticker is drawn into the image pixels, so it cannot be moved afterward except by using Undo.

Open Object Add

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

Emoji are rendered with the fonts installed on your device, so the same sticker can look different on Windows, macOS, Android and iOS. The tool does not synthesize photorealistic objects.

When to use Object Add

  • You are making a lighthearted greeting, party invitation or social post.
  • You want a quick marker, such as a star or arrow, on a screenshot or diagram.
  • You need to cover a small area, like a face in a group photo, with a graphic.

Step by step

  1. Upload the image and open Object Add.
  2. Choose a sticker from the list.
  3. Set the horizontal and vertical position and the size, using the sliders in the panel.
  4. Press Place Sticker and check the position in the preview.
  5. Repeat for additional stickers or undo and adjust. Download when you are satisfied.

Tips for better results

  • Percentages are relative to the image, so the same settings produce the same relative position on photos of different sizes.
  • Keep stickers away from faces and important text unless you intend to cover them.
  • Use a larger size for images you will display small, such as profile pictures, so that the graphic remains readable.
  • Preview on the device where the image will be viewed if the exact look of the emoji matters.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the sticker is a separate layer that can be moved later. Use Undo and place it again.
  • Placing stickers near the edge where they may be cut off when the image is cropped later.
  • Expecting realistic objects such as furniture or animals.

What to expect

Emoji appearance depends on device fonts. This does not synthesize photorealistic objects.

Place a celebration sticker near a corner, keeping it clear of faces and important text.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Models and libraries, where a tool needs them, download from public content networks. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Questions about Object Add

Can I upload my own sticker or logo?

Not in this tool. For a logo or custom graphic, use Watermark Maker, which accepts a logo image and lets you set opacity and position.

Why does the sticker look different on another device?

Emoji are drawn from the system font. Different platforms ship different artwork for the same character.

How many stickers can I add?

There is no fixed count. Each application draws one sticker, and you can apply as many times as you like until the editor's undo history limit is reached.
