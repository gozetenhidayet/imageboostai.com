AI Outpainting

AI Outpainting enlarges the canvas around your photo. The new border is filled by mirroring the edge pixels of the image outward and blurring them, while the original picture stays untouched in the center. You choose how much to extend, from 5% to 50% with a default of 20%, and the extension is added on both sides of each dimension.

Open AI Outpainting

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

For example, a 1000 × 800 image extended by 20% becomes 1400 × 1120 pixels. No new scene content is generated, so the border is a soft continuation of what is already at the edges rather than a plausible extension of the scene.

When to use AI Outpainting

  • You need more empty space around a subject so you can add text or fit a particular aspect ratio.
  • A sky, wall or studio backdrop at the edge of the photo can reasonably be continued.
  • You want a soft blurred frame around a picture without leaving a flat color border.

Step by step

  1. Upload the image and open AI Outpainting.
  2. Set the extension percentage with the slider. Remember that it is applied to both sides.
  3. Press Extend Canvas and look at the border at 100% zoom.
  4. If recognizable shapes repeat near the edge, undo and use a smaller percentage.
  5. Crop afterward if you need a specific ratio, then download.

Tips for better results

  • Choose photos with simple edges, such as sky, water, blurred backgrounds or plain walls.
  • Use a small extension, around 5 to 15%, when you only need breathing room for a title.
  • Because the extension is mirrored, a person standing at the edge will be reflected. Avoid this on busy edges.
  • To reach an exact ratio, extend first and then use Crop Image.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting new buildings, people or landscape to appear in the border.
  • Using a very large extension on a busy photo. Repeated mirrored patterns become obvious above about 30%.
  • Forgetting that the final pixel dimensions increase, which affects file size.

What to expect

No new scene content is generated. Reflections can repeat recognizable objects near the edges.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about AI Outpainting

Does it generate new parts of the scene?

No. It continues edge pixels by mirroring and blurring. It cannot imagine content that was never in the photo.

How do I calculate the new image size?

Multiply each dimension by 1 plus twice the percentage. At 20%, 1 + 0.2 × 2 = 1.4, so a 1000 × 800 image becomes 1400 × 1120.

Can I extend only one side?

Not in this tool. The extension is symmetric. Crop afterward if you want an asymmetric result.
