Remove Background

Foreground cutout

Remove Background separates a subject from its surroundings and returns the subject on a transparent canvas.

Background Changer

Background replacement

Background Changer takes the subject cutout produced by the same detection step as Remove Background and places it over a new backdrop.

AI Background Generator

Background presets

Despite its name, AI Background Generator is a preset background studio.

Object Remover

Local area repair

Object Remover fills the area you select using colors from the pixels around it.

Object Replace

Selected-area recoloring

Object Replace recolors a region you select.

Object Add

Sticker placement

Object Add places an emoji sticker on your image.

AI Inpainting

Selected-area fill

AI Inpainting is the same selected-area repair engine that powers Object Remover.

AI Outpainting

Canvas extension

AI Outpainting enlarges the canvas around your photo.

Magic Eraser

Brush repair

AI Photo Restore

Photo cleanup

Colorize Old Photo

Manual tint

Colorize Old Photo applies a single tint across the whole picture.

Image Upscaler

Image enlargement

Image Upscaler makes a picture larger by resampling it at 2×, 4× or 8× of its current width and height.

Image Sharpener

Edge enhancement

Image Sharpener increases edge contrast with an unsharp mask.

Noise Remover

Noise reduction

Noise Remover reduces grain by blending a blurred copy of the image into the original.

Photo Enhancer

Automatic tonal adjustment

Photo Enhancer is a one-click tonal adjustment with no settings.

Color Correction

Color adjustment

Color Correction gives you five sliders, each from −100 to +100, plus an Auto-Enhance button.

Brightness & Contrast

Exposure and tone

Brightness & Contrast offers three independent sliders, Brightness, Contrast and Saturation, each from 0% to 200%.

Remove Blur

Clarity sharpening

Remove Blur applies a fixed-strength local contrast sharpening to soften-looking images.

Face Retouch

Selected skin retouch

Face Retouch softens and slightly brightens only the area you paint.

Skin Smoothing

Skin and blemish controls

Skin Smoothing has two parts.

Teeth Whitening

Selected brightening

Teeth Whitening brightens and de-yellows the area you paint.

Eye Enhancer

Selected eye adjustment

Eye Enhancer makes the painted region more vivid.

AI Headshot

Portrait styling

AI Headshot is a portrait-styling shortcut that uses ordinary image processing and a subject cutout, not a generative model.

Portrait Enhancer

Portrait tone and texture

Portrait Enhancer is a one-click adjustment for photos of people.

Blur Background

Depth effect

Blur Background keeps your subject sharp and blurs everything behind it.

Crop Image

Crop, rotate and flip

Crop Image lets you cut a photo to a chosen shape.

Resize Image

Exact dimensions

Resize Image changes the pixel dimensions of a photo.

Image Compressor

JPEG compression

Image Compressor saves your picture as a JPEG at a quality you choose.

Convert Image

PNG, JPEG and WebP export

Convert Image exports your current picture as PNG, JPG or WebP.

Selected mark repair

Watermark Maker

Text or logo watermark

Watermark Maker places visible text or a logo on your image.

Add Text to Photo

Text overlay

Add Text to Photo draws a line of text on your image.

Image to Text OCR

Local text recognition

Image to Text OCR reads printed text from a picture and gives it back as editable text.

Product Photo Editor

Product presentation

Product Photo Editor wraps your picture in a clean presentation layout.

Photo Collage Maker

Multi-image collage

Photo Collage Maker combines your current image with up to eight additional JPG, PNG or WebP files into one grid.
