Photo Enhancer

Photo Enhancer is a one-click tonal adjustment with no settings. It examines each color channel of your image, finds the values below which 1% of the pixels fall and above which 1% of the pixels fall, and stretches that range toward the full 0 to 255 scale. It then blends 65% of that stretched result with 35% of the original, so the correction is restrained. Fully transparent pixels are ignored in the calculation, and a channel whose range is already very narrow is left alone.

The effect is a gentle increase in contrast and a mild neutralizing of color casts. It is not a trained enhancement model. It does not reconstruct detail, and because it calculates on percentiles, flat-color graphics such as logos stay essentially unchanged rather than turning black or washed out.

When to use Photo Enhancer

  • A photo looks flat or hazy and needs a quick improvement without choosing settings.
  • You want a starting point before fine-tuning exposure or color.
  • You process many similar photos and want a consistent, mild correction.

Step by step

  1. Upload the photo and open Photo Enhancer.
  2. Press Apply Tool once.
  3. Switch between the original and edited previews, looking at shadows, highlights and skin tones.
  4. If contrast is already strong in the original and the result looks harsh, use Undo.
  5. Download the result, or continue with Color Correction for finer control.

Tips for better results

  • The tool works on whole-image statistics. A photo dominated by one color, such as a sunset, may shift in ways you did not intend.
  • Because it blends 65% of the corrected image, a second pass applies the same restrained correction again. Check for clipping after the second pass.
  • Transparent PNG cutouts are handled correctly, since transparent pixels are excluded from the measurement.
  • For product images with pure white backgrounds, check that the background has not turned slightly gray.

Common mistakes

  • Applying it to an already well-exposed image, which can push contrast too far.
  • Expecting it to repair out-of-focus or noisy photos. It only changes tone and color balance.
  • Applying it repeatedly in an attempt to increase the effect. Use Brightness & Contrast for a controlled adjustment.

What to expect

This is a tonal adjustment, not a trained enhancement model. Flat-color graphics are intentionally preserved.

A low-contrast photograph can gain separation without turning a uniform colored logo black.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about Photo Enhancer

Is this an AI enhancer?

No. It is an automatic levels adjustment based on percentiles. That makes it predictable and fast, but it only changes tone and color balance.

Why does the tool have no sliders?

It is designed as a quick one-click fix. For manual control use Color Correction, which has exposure, shadows, highlights, temperature and tint sliders.

Will it change a logo with flat colors?

Very little. When a channel contains only a narrow range of values the tool leaves it untouched, which keeps flat graphics from changing color.
