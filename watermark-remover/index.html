Watermark Remover

Watermark Remover uses the same selected-area repair engine as Object Remover: you brush over a mark and the tool fills it from the surrounding pixels. It has a Brush, an Eraser and a Clear button, and a Remove button. Nothing is uploaded; the repair runs in your browser.

Open Watermark Remover

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

Use it only on marks you are entitled to remove, such as a date stamp the camera added to your own photo or a watermark you applied yourself. It is not meant for removing ownership notices from someone else's work, which can infringe copyright. Technically, it cannot recover the pixels hidden beneath a watermark. It estimates a fill from the area around it, which works for small marks and plain backgrounds and fails for large, semi-transparent logos across detailed content.

When to use Watermark Remover

  • A camera date stamp sits on a plain area of your own photo.
  • You exported your own image with a watermark by mistake and have no clean copy.
  • A small scanning label or note needs to be cleaned from a document image you own.

Step by step

  1. Upload the image and open Watermark Remover.
  2. Zoom to the mark, choose a small brush, and paint over it narrowly. Include the full mark but not much of the surrounding detail.
  3. Press Remove and inspect the edges around the repaired area.
  4. If the result is smeared, use Undo and repeat with smaller strokes, one section at a time.
  5. Download the image once the repair is not visible at normal size.

Tips for better results

  • The best case is a mark on a smooth surface, such as sky, wall or a solid-colored backdrop.
  • Text marks are easier to repair than large logos because they cover less area.
  • Do several short passes instead of one big one. Averaged fill from nearby pixels works over short distances.
  • Keep an unedited copy. If you need a clean image for a commercial purpose, obtain the original without the watermark.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to remove a stock-photo watermark. That is typically a license violation, and the tool cannot restore the image properly anyway.
  • Painting the whole mark with a very large brush. The fill becomes a smooth blur.
  • Expecting the original content under the watermark to appear. It is estimated, not recovered.

What to expect

This cannot recover original pixels hidden beneath a large mark. Do not remove ownership notices without permission.

A small accidental date stamp on your own plain-background photo is more suitable than a large logo over a face.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Models and libraries, where a tool needs them, download from public content networks. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Questions about Watermark Remover

Is it legal to remove a watermark?

It depends on who owns the image and what rights you have. Removing a watermark from an image you do not own, in order to use it without permission, can infringe copyright. Use this tool only on images you own or are authorized to edit.

Why does the area look smudged after removal?

The tool fills the area by averaging colors from its surroundings. On detailed backgrounds this produces a blur.

Is this the same engine as Object Remover?

Yes. The repair method is identical. This panel only has a Remove button and a more specific hint.
