Crop Image

Crop Image lets you cut a photo to a chosen shape. The panel offers six quick options: 1:1 (square), 16:9, 4:3, 9:16, 3:2 and Original. You can also drag a custom rectangle on the picture and press Crop Selected Area. Below the crop controls, four buttons rotate the image 90° or 180° and flip it horizontally or vertically.

Open Crop Image

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

Cropping permanently removes the pixels outside the selection. It does not resize the remaining image and it does not rebuild content outside the frame. The preview coordinates are scaled to the displayed image, so the crop you see matches the crop applied to the full-resolution file.

When to use Crop Image

  • You need a square image for a profile picture or a grid post.
  • A vertical 9:16 crop is needed for a story or short video cover.
  • You want to straighten the orientation of a photo that was saved sideways, or mirror it.

Step by step

  1. Upload the image and open Crop Image.
  2. Click a ratio, or drag a rectangle over the part of the picture you want to keep.
  3. Move or adjust the selection so the subject, especially faces, is well inside the frame.
  4. Press Crop Selected Area. Use Undo if you want to try a different crop.
  5. Rotate or flip if needed, then download the result.

Tips for better results

  • Leave margin around the subject. Social platforms and layouts often crop again, and tight crops lose the subject.
  • Follow the rule of thirds: place the subject near one of the thirds lines rather than dead center for a more dynamic result.
  • Crop first and resize afterward. Resizing before cropping wastes pixels you are about to remove.
  • Check the final dimensions in the status line to make sure the image still has enough pixels for its use.

Common mistakes

  • Cropping too tightly around a face, which leaves no space if the platform adds its own crop or mask.
  • Choosing a ratio, such as 9:16, from a horizontal photo and losing most of the picture.
  • Cropping repeatedly in small steps. Each crop reduces resolution, so decide on the final frame in one move.

What to expect

Cropping removes pixels; it does not resize or reconstruct content outside the selection.

Use 1:1 for a square avatar or 9:16 for a vertical social image, keeping the face inside the crop.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about Crop Image

Does cropping reduce quality?

It does not blur or compress the remaining pixels, but it reduces the number of pixels. If you later enlarge the crop, it will look softer.

How do I crop to an exact pixel size?

Crop to the correct ratio first, then use Resize Image to set the exact width and height.

What does Original do?

It uses the photo's own proportions, so the selection is not reshaped. Pick another ratio or drag a custom area when you want a different shape.
