Teeth Whitening

Teeth Whitening brightens and de-yellows the area you paint. Inside the painted selection, the tool blends 85% of a filtered copy into the original. The filter raises brightness by 25%, reduces color saturation to 60% and adds a 5% contrast lift, which lowers the yellow tint while keeping some natural variation between teeth.

Open Teeth Whitening

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

The brush runs from 8 to 90 on the preview scale (default 28), with an Eraser and a Clear button. The tool does not detect teeth. Anything you paint receives the same treatment, so lips, gums and skin that are included by mistake will also turn paler and grayer.

When to use Teeth Whitening

  • A smile photo has a slight yellow cast on the teeth caused by indoor lighting.
  • You want to even out the brightness of teeth in a portrait for a profile photo.
  • You want a subtle improvement without changing the rest of the face.

Step by step

  1. Upload the portrait and zoom the view so the teeth are large on screen.
  2. Open Teeth Whitening and choose a small brush, around 8 to 15 on the size slider for a front smile.
  3. Paint only on the teeth and avoid gums, lips and the dark gaps between teeth.
  4. Press Apply and compare the smile at normal viewing size. If it looks unnatural, use Undo.
  5. Download the result.

Tips for better results

  • Keep the effect modest. Teeth that are brighter than the whites of the eyes look artificial.
  • Use the Eraser to clean the edges of the selection near the gums rather than painting very carefully in one pass.
  • Compare with the eye whites and shirt collar in the same photo; teeth should not be the brightest object.
  • Apply once. A second pass raises brightness again and can remove natural shading.

Common mistakes

  • Painting over lips or gums, which become pale and gray.
  • Using a large brush that covers the dark spaces between teeth, flattening the depth of the smile.
  • Applying the tool twice in an attempt to get a stronger effect.

What to expect

The tool does not detect teeth automatically. Overpainting changes surrounding colors.

A careful mask can reduce a yellow cast without making the lips pale.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Models and libraries, where a tool needs them, download from public content networks. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Questions about Teeth Whitening

Does the tool find teeth automatically?

No. It changes whatever you paint, so careful selection is the most important step.

How strong is the whitening?

Within the painted area, 85% of a copy with 25% more brightness and reduced saturation is blended in. There is no slider, so use a smaller selection or Undo to reduce the effect.

Is it a dental or cosmetic treatment tool?

No. It edits the colors of pixels in a photograph and has no connection to real teeth whitening products or treatments.
