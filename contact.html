Contact ImageBoostAI

We read every message about a tool problem, an incorrect description, a privacy concern or a copyright notice.

Email contact@imageboostai.com

This opens your email application. This page does not submit a form or send a message automatically, and nothing you type here leaves your browser until you send the email yourself.

What to write about

  • A tool problem. Something does not work, or works differently from its guide.
  • A correction. A guide contains a mistake, an out-of-date number or an unclear explanation. We check the tool and fix the guide.
  • A privacy question. See the privacy policy first, then ask us anything it does not answer.
  • A copyright concern. Include the page address, the work concerned and how to reach you. See the terms of use.
  • A suggestion. A tool you would like, or a topic for a guide.

What helps us reproduce a problem

  • The name of the tool and the steps you followed, including the settings you chose.
  • Your browser and device, for example Chrome on Windows, or Safari on an iPhone.
  • The input format and the approximate pixel size of the image.
  • The exact message shown in the status line, if any, and whether a smaller image works.

Please use a non-sensitive sample image instead of sending private documents or identifiable photographs. Do not email passwords or API keys.

Before you write

  • Check the guide for the tool. Many questions about limits, sizes and settings are answered there, and the guides page lists them all.
  • If a download fails, check your connection. The background tools and OCR download a model or library the first time you use them.
  • We cannot recover a file that was closed without being downloaded, because images are never stored on our side.

Replies

We answer as soon as we can, and a reply is not guaranteed for every message. Corrections to the guides are made once we can confirm them, and the guide’s review date is updated when we do.
