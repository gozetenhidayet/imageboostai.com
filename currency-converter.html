Privacy and local processing

Toolnova is designed so this feature can run in the browser. This reduces the need to send documents to a Toolnova server and makes the workflow practical on phones, tablets and desktop browsers. Avoid using any online utility with documents you are not authorized to process.

Important limitation: Rates are fetched live but refresh periodically (not tick-by-tick). For an actual transaction — a wire transfer, a purchase — always confirm the exact rate your bank or payment provider will use, since they may add their own markup.

Frequently asked questions

Does Currency Converter upload my input to Toolnova?

The tool is designed to process the selected input in your browser. Third-party JavaScript libraries may be downloaded to your device so the feature can run, but Toolnova does not intentionally upload the selected document to a Toolnova server.

Is Currency Converter free?

Yes. This Toolnova feature can be used without creating an account or starting a subscription.

What should I check after using it?

For a transaction you're actually completing (a wire transfer, a purchase), always confirm the exact rate your bank or payment provider will use — it may include a markup or fee not reflected in the reference rate shown here.
