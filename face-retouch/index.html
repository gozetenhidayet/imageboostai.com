Face Retouch

Face Retouch softens and slightly brightens only the area you paint. You mark skin with a round brush (size 8 to 90 on the preview scale, default 28) and press Apply. Inside the painted area the tool blends 75% of a filtered copy into the original, where the filter is a 3-pixel blur combined with a 5% brightness lift and a small reduction in contrast. Pixels outside the brush are untouched.

Open Face Retouch

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

There is no face detection. The tool does not know where eyes, lips or hairlines are, so the quality of the result depends entirely on your selection. That is useful because you stay in control, and it is the reason to keep the brush away from eyelashes, eyebrows, lips, teeth and strands of hair, which would turn soft and blurry.

When to use Face Retouch

  • You want to soften shine, fine lines or uneven skin texture on cheeks, forehead or chin.
  • A portrait looks too harsh because of flash or strong lighting.
  • You want a lighter touch than a whole-image skin filter, applied only where it is needed.

Step by step

  1. Upload the portrait and open Face Retouch.
  2. Set the brush size to match the area you want to treat. Use a larger brush for cheeks and a smaller one for the forehead edge.
  3. Paint over skin only. Use the Eraser to remove paint from areas you covered by mistake, or Clear to start over.
  4. Press Apply and compare the result with the original at the size it will be viewed.
  5. If the skin looks too smooth, press Undo and treat a smaller area, then download.

Tips for better results

  • Retouch lightly. Natural skin has texture, and removing all of it gives a plastic look that viewers notice immediately.
  • Paint in a few short strokes and check, rather than covering the entire face in one go.
  • Keep the brush away from the edges of the face where skin meets hair and background, to avoid blurry outlines.
  • Do not apply it twice to the same area unless you are sure. The blur and brightening add up.

Common mistakes

  • Painting over eyes, eyebrows, lips or teeth, which blurs the features that give a face its character.
  • Using a very large brush that crosses hair or background and creates a halo.
  • Expecting the tool to remove spots entirely. It softens them. For single blemishes use Skin Smoothing's Heal Spot.

What to expect

There is no automatic face landmark detection. The selection determines which pixels change.

Retouch a cheek without softening the eyelashes or hairline.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about Face Retouch

Does the tool detect faces automatically?

No. It changes only the pixels you paint, so you decide exactly where the effect applies.

How strong is the effect?

Inside the painted area, 75% of a blurred and slightly brightened copy is blended in. There is no strength slider, so use Undo and smaller selections to reduce the effect.

Is it suitable for professional portraits?

It is a quick, simple softening tool. For detailed professional retouching, including frequency separation, use a dedicated editor.
