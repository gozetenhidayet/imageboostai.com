AI Photo Restore

AI Photo Restore is a one-click cleanup for faded or slightly grainy scans, and it uses classical image processing rather than a trained restoration model. It runs four steps in order. First it blends about 28% of a lightly blurred copy back into the image to calm film grain and scanner noise. Next it stretches each color channel from its darkest to its brightest value, which restores contrast to a washed-out print. It then nudges the balance slightly away from red and toward blue, which makes yellowed prints look more neutral. Finally it applies mild sharpening.

Open AI Photo Restore

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

There are no settings to adjust and the tool does not detect scratches, tears or faces. It cannot rebuild missing detail. For narrow scratches, follow it with Object Remover; for color, use Color Correction or Colorize Old Photo.

When to use AI Photo Restore

  • A scan of a print looks flat, hazy or slightly yellow and needs more contrast.
  • An old photograph has visible grain that you want to soften a little before sharing it.
  • You want a quick improvement before doing selective repairs on cracks or marks.

Step by step

  1. Scan or photograph the print as flat and evenly lit as you can, and crop away black scanner borders before uploading.
  2. Upload the image and open AI Photo Restore, then press Apply Tool.
  3. Compare faces, shadow areas and paper texture with the original, using the before-and-after view.
  4. If the result is too strong or too cool, use Undo and finish with gentler adjustments in Color Correction instead.
  5. Download the result as PNG, or compress it afterward if you need a smaller file.

Tips for better results

  • The contrast stretch uses the actual darkest and brightest pixel in each channel, so a single black scanner edge or a white speck limits the improvement. Crop first.
  • Scan at 300 DPI or higher for prints you plan to enlarge. The tool improves tone, not resolution.
  • If the print has a strong color cast, correct that with the temperature and tint sliders after restoring contrast.
  • Keep the untouched scan. Restoration steps are not reversible once you export.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting the tool to repair tears, stains or missing corners. Those need selective repair or manual work.
  • Applying it several times in a row. Each pass stretches contrast and adds blur and sharpening again, which can clip highlights.
  • Running it on a photo that is already high in contrast. The channel stretch has little room to improve and the blue shift may be noticeable.

What to expect

This is classical photo processing. It cannot recreate missing facial detail or automatically reconstruct major damage.

A faded scan may gain contrast; torn areas still require careful selected-area repair.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Questions about AI Photo Restore

Does this use artificial intelligence?

No. The name is a legacy label. The steps are noise blending, a per-channel levels stretch, a small color shift and mild sharpening, all computed on your device with standard image math.

Why did my photo become slightly cooler?

The tool reduces red slightly and increases blue slightly to counter the yellow cast typical of aged prints. If your photo was already neutral or cool, correct the balance afterward with the Temperature slider in Color Correction.

Can it fix a cracked or torn photo?

Not by itself. It improves overall tone. Narrow cracks can be blended with Object Remover, but missing areas cannot be reconstructed.
