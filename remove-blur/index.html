Remove Blur

Remove Blur applies a fixed-strength local contrast sharpening to soften-looking images. It blurs a copy of the picture by 2.2 pixels, finds the difference between that copy and your image, and adds the difference back at 1.35 times its size. There are no sliders; you press Apply Tool and compare the result.

Open Remove Blur

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

Despite the name, it cannot reverse real blur. Motion blur and missed focus destroy information that no filter can bring back. The tool works as a sharpening step for mildly soft images, such as scans, screenshots after resizing, or photos that were slightly softened by compression.

When to use Remove Blur

  • A photo is slightly soft but acceptable, and a modest improvement is enough.
  • A scan or screenshot of text looks fuzzy at the edges.
  • You want a quick sharpening step and do not need to choose a strength.

Step by step

  1. Upload the image and open Remove Blur.
  2. Press Apply Tool once.
  3. Compare with the original at 100% zoom, looking at edges of text, eyes and fine textures.
  4. Use Undo if halos appear, and try Image Sharpener instead, which lets you choose a lower strength.
  5. Download the result.

Tips for better results

  • If you want to control the amount, use Image Sharpener, which has a strength slider. Remove Blur always uses one fixed strength.
  • Check for bright outlines around dark text or objects, which indicate the effect was strong for your image.
  • Sharpen after resizing. Enlarging the image afterward softens the edges again.
  • Do not apply it twice on a noisy image. The noise will be amplified.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting a motion-blurred photo to become sharp. The detail is not in the file.
  • Using it on high-ISO photos with visible grain, which makes the grain more noticeable.
  • Applying it repeatedly instead of switching to a tool with an adjustable strength.

What to expect

This is sharpening, not deconvolution or generative detail recovery. Severe blur remains a limitation.

A slightly soft scan may improve; a moving subject with a long smear will not become a sharp portrait.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Models and libraries, where a tool needs them, download from public content networks. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Questions about Remove Blur

Can it fix a blurry photo?

Only a little. It increases edge contrast, so slightly soft images look crisper. Heavy blur from motion or focus cannot be reversed.

How is this different from Image Sharpener?

They use the same kind of unsharp mask. Remove Blur uses one fixed setting for one-click use, while Image Sharpener lets you choose the strength from 0 to 100%.

Why do my edges have light outlines now?

That is a halo from over-sharpening. Use Undo, then try Image Sharpener with a lower value.
