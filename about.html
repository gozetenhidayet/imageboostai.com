About ImageBoostAI

ImageBoostAI is a free, browser-based photo editor with 35 tools for backgrounds, retouching, enhancement, resizing, conversion and text recognition. This page explains why it exists, how the tools work, and how we keep the descriptions honest.

Why this site exists

Many online photo tools ask you to upload your picture to a server, create an account, or accept a watermark on the result. ImageBoostAI was built so that everyday edits can be done in the browser instead. Your image is processed on your own device, there is no sign-up, and the site does not add a watermark to your downloads.

The aim is practical: help someone remove a distracting background, shrink a photo for an upload form, extract text from a screenshot or prepare a clean product picture, and explain clearly what each step does so the result is predictable.

What you can do here

  • Background tools: remove or change a background, blur it, and repair or erase small marks.
  • Restore and fix: sharpen, reduce noise, correct color and tone, enlarge, and clean up scans.
  • Face and portrait: brush-based skin softening, teeth and eye adjustments, and a simple portrait styling tool.
  • Convert and resize: crop, resize (including nominal photo-size presets for twelve countries), compress and convert between PNG, JPG and WebP.
  • Design: add text, watermarks and stickers, build collages, create product-style cards, and read text from images with OCR.

Every tool has its own guide page that explains what it does, how to use it, its settings and its limits. You can find them all on the guides page.

How the tools really work

Some tool names include the word “AI” because people search for them that way. In fact, six tools use a trained recognition model that runs in your browser (five for finding the subject of a photo and one for reading text), and none of the 35 tools generates new image content. The rest use ordinary image-processing math. Each guide states the real method, and the article What “AI” means in photo tools lists them all.

How we write and check our guides

  • The settings, ranges, defaults and limits in a guide come from the tool itself. We run the tool and read the controls and the code rather than rely on assumptions.
  • We prefer plain statements of limits over promises. If a tool cannot do something, the guide says so.
  • Numbers in worked examples are calculated, not estimated, and where results depend on the photo we say that.
  • When a tool changes, we update its guide and its “reviewed” date.

If you find a mistake, a mismatch between a guide and the tool, or something unclear, please tell us. Corrections are made as soon as we can confirm them.

How the site is funded

ImageBoostAI is free to use. To help cover running costs the site may display advertising, for example through Google AdSense, and it can use optional analytics. Advertising and analytics scripts load only after you give consent where consent is required, and advertising never changes how a tool works or what it produces. Your images are not sold, shared or uploaded for editing. See the privacy policy for the full list of third-party services.

Responsible use

Use the tools only on images you own or have permission to edit. Do not remove ownership marks from other people’s work, misrepresent edited images as unedited, or alter identity documents. Read more in the terms of use.
