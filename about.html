About ImageBoostAI

Practical photo tools for background editing, image adjustments, design, conversion and OCR.

The editor combines conventional image-processing algorithms with optional local models for foreground segmentation and text recognition. Each tool guide explains its actual method and limits. A legacy “AI” name does not mean that a feature generates new content.

What you can expect

Core tools are free and do not require an account. Adjustments apply to your current image. Undo and redo are held only in the current browser session. Keep originals and download finished files before leaving.

How we describe quality

Image quality depends on the source and the operation. Enlarging an image adds pixels; it does not recover missing detail. A local repair can blend a small mark into its surroundings; it cannot reconstruct a complex scene. Our guides make these distinctions so you can choose the right workflow.

Report a reproducible problem with the tool name, browser and settings. Avoid sending personal or sensitive photographs.
