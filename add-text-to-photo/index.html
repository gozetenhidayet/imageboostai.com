Add Text to Photo

Add Text to Photo draws a line of text on your image. Type the words, choose a font (Arial, Georgia, Impact, Courier New or Times New Roman), set the color and size, and optionally turn on Bold, Italic or Underline. Drag the text on the preview to position it, then press Add Text. Repeat the process to add another independent line.

Open Add Text to Photo

No account required. Keep your original and review the result before sharing.

Two styles are available. Solid Color draws the text in your chosen color with a thin dark outline so that it stays readable on busy backgrounds. Photo Fill is different: the letters show your photo through them while everything else becomes a near-black background, which makes a bold graphic title. The size is measured in real pixels of your image, from 14 to 140, so on a large photo you will want a higher value than on a small one. The text becomes part of the picture; there is no editable text layer.

When to use Add Text to Photo

  • You want a caption, title or short message on a photo for a social post or card.
  • You want a graphic text effect where the photo shows inside large letters.
  • You need to label an image, such as a date or location, in a clear color.

Step by step

  1. Upload the image and open Add Text to Photo.
  2. Type your text and choose Solid Color or Photo Fill.
  3. Pick a font, color and size. Add Bold, Italic or Underline if you like.
  4. Drag the text on the small preview to where you want it, then press Add Text.
  5. Check the result, use Undo to adjust, and download.

Tips for better results

  • Size is in image pixels. A 48-pixel caption looks large on an 800-pixel-wide image and tiny on a 4000-pixel photo, so scale it to your image.
  • Place text over a calm, dark area and leave a margin so that it survives later cropping.
  • For Photo Fill, use a short word in a heavy font such as Impact, with a large size. Thin fonts leave too little of the photo visible.
  • Contrast matters more than color choice. White with the automatic dark outline works on most photos.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting the text to be re-editable later. Use Undo and add it again.
  • Using Photo Fill with a long sentence and a small size, which makes the photo hard to see.
  • Placing text near the edge, where it will be cut off if the picture is cropped for another platform.

What to expect

Text becomes pixels in the image. Fonts vary by device and applied text is edited through Undo, not a saved text layer.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Models and libraries, where a tool needs them, download from public content networks. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Questions about Add Text to Photo

What does Photo Fill do exactly?

It draws your text, fills the letters with your photo, and fills the rest of the image with a near-black color. The result is your picture seen only through the shape of the text.

Why does my text look so small?

The size is in pixels of the actual image. A large photo needs a larger value. Increase Size up to 140.

Can I add multiple lines of text?

Add one line at a time. Press Add Text, then enter new text, position it and press Add Text again. Each application becomes part of the image.
