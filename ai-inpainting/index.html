AI Inpainting

AI Inpainting is the same selected-area repair engine that powers Object Remover. You brush or lasso a damaged or unwanted region, and the tool fills it by spreading colors inward from the surrounding pixels. It does not use a generative model, so it cannot imagine what should be behind a missing object.

We kept the familiar name for people searching for inpainting, but the interface and this page describe the real method. Think of it as a precise smoothing patch for small defects such as scratches, dust or ink marks on a scan.

When to use AI Inpainting

  • A scanned photo has a thin crack, a crease line or a few dust spots.
  • A screenshot or document image has a small mark you need to blend out.
  • You want to repair a narrow area on a smooth sky or wall.

Step by step

  1. Upload the image and open AI Inpainting.
  2. Brush (or outline with the Lasso) a narrow mask over the defect. Keep the mask only slightly wider than the damage.
  3. Press Remove Selected Area and inspect the repaired texture at full size.
  4. Undo and shrink the mask if the patch looks too smooth or smeared.
  5. Repeat on other defects one at a time, then download.

Tips for better results

  • Several short, narrow masks beat one large mask. The algorithm copies from nearby pixels only.
  • Repair defects on flat areas first, where the result is nearly invisible.
  • After repairing a scan, a mild sharpening or noise pass can bring the patch texture closer to the surroundings.
  • Always keep the original file in case you need to start over.

Common mistakes

  • Masking an entire face or a large part of the picture. The tool cannot reconstruct facial features.
  • Painting over high-contrast edges such as text or hair, where averaging colors creates visible smudges.
  • Expecting the fill to match grain or fabric texture.

What to expect

The legacy AI name does not imply generative inpainting. Large missing objects cannot be reconstructed reliably.

Use a narrow mask for a small scan defect rather than covering an entire face.

Input, export and privacy

The workspace accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and HEIC images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels; some devices need smaller files. Your image is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Models and libraries, where a tool needs them, download from public content networks. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Questions about AI Inpainting

Is AI Inpainting different from Object Remover?

The engine is the same. The panels differ only in wording, so use whichever name you know.

Can it rebuild a torn corner of a photograph?

No. It can blend narrow damage, but it cannot invent missing content. A torn corner needs manual restoration in a dedicated editor.

What size of mask works best?

Masks only a few pixels wider than the defect work best. Very large masks produce a flat color area.
